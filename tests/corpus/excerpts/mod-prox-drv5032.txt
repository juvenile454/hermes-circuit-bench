The DU, DG, FA, FB, and FD device versions have push-pull CMOS outputs that can drive a V CC or ground
level. The FC, AJ, and ZE device versions have open-drain outputs that can become high impedance or drive
ground. For these versions, an external pullup resistor must be used.
