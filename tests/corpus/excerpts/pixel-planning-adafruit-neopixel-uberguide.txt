Each individual NeoPixel draws up to 60 milliamps at maximum brightness white (red + green + blue).
It’s impossible to estimate a single number for all circumstances, but we’ve been using 1/3 this (20 mA per pixel) as a gross rule of thumb with no ill effects.
But if you know for a fact that you need every pixel on at maximum brightness, use the full 60 mA figure.
60 NeoPixels × 60 mA ÷ 1,000 = 3.6 Amps minimum
Keep in mind, 60 mA is a worst case estimate!
